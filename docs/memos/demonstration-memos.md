# Demonstration memo set

These are fictional exercises from the seeded universe. None is a statement about a listed company. The live company workbench produces the current numerical draft from its actual scenario; the notes here are qualitative questions to investigate.

## 1. Cedar Castings (SIM016)

Sample thesis: recurring industrial replacement demand may support debt reduction if maintenance capital is fully funded. Underwrite with no multiple expansion. Open demo-16 and examine the source-linked EBITDA adjustment before accepting it. Diligence: casting yield losses, energy costs, customer concentration and whether working capital releases recur. Pass if the underwriting depends on underfunded maintenance capex.

## 2. Triveni Components (SIM008)

Sample thesis: a specialist component supplier can be attractive when customer qualification makes replacement difficult. The model's debt repayment, not a terminal re-rating, should explain the return. Diligence: aftermarket share, contract renewal terms, tooling ownership and concentration. Pass if the revenue growth required by the hurdle solver cannot be supported by orders.

## 3. Kaveri Polymers (SIM032)

Sample thesis: formulation know-how may protect gross profit, but inventory and raw-material cycles can consume apparent cash earnings. Run the low-growth scenario and verify cash conversion in the historical table. Diligence: feedstock pass-through, environment liabilities and procurement concentration. Pass if normalized earnings exclude recurring compliance costs.

## 4. Meridian Motion (SIM024)

Sample thesis: precision motion components may have sticky industrial applications. Test flat exit multiples and the 20% EBITDA decline. Diligence: export currency exposures, customer capex cycles and installed-base spares. Pass if the cash sweep requires inventory turns beyond the historical range.

## 5. Aster Polymers (SIM031)

Sample thesis: a constrained entry price can make a stable materials business investable without aggressive financing. Compare the entry/exit grid to the leverage/growth grid. Diligence: maintenance shutdowns, capacity utilization and contract profitability. Pass if nominal EBITDA growth requires a disproportionate increase in working capital.
